Dogear is one package that adds comments to a website or a coded prototype. Click to drop a pin or drag to mark an area, then write. You get threads, replies, edits, resolve and live updates, in plain HTML, React and Next.js.

Use it for design reviews on a staging site, for feedback from visitors on a live one, or to hand what people said straight to your coding agent.

It's open source under the MIT licence. Comments live on your own backend, with no Dogear service in between.

## What you get

- **Pins that stay put.** A comment is anchored to the element under it, so it follows that element through scrolling, resizing and layout changes. See [how pins stay put](/concepts/pins).
- **Threads.** Reply, @mention a teammate, react, edit and resolve, with Undo.
- **Your own backend.** One route handler over Postgres, a JSON file or memory, or a five-function adapter over anything else. With no backend at all, comments stay in the browser.
- **Guests without accounts.** Visitors give a name once and comment, under a cookie your server signs.
- **A look that fits.** Dark or light glass, or the visitor's system setting, with every colour open to your CSS.
- **Agents in the loop.** `dogear mcp` hands threads to Claude Code, Cursor or Codex, and their replies show on the page.
